# IEEE OCEANS 2026 (Monterey) — Poster Submission: ACCEPTED

**Status:** Accepted.
**Venue:** IEEE OCEANS 2026, Monterey, California.
**Format:** Poster-only (no paper / no camera-ready manuscript required for this submission).
**Authors:** Duane R. Edgington and John Ryan (MBARI).
**Topic:** Marine soundscape / passive acoustic monitoring science — detecting and
distinguishing marine mammal vocalizations (Bigg's orca, Pacific white-sided dolphin,
humpback whale) and vessel/ship noise on the MARS cabled hydrophone (Monterey Bay), using
transfer learning on Perch 2.0 embeddings via the agile-modeling (perch-hoplite) workflow.
